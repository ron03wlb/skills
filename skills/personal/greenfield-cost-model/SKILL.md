# Greenfield Cost Model

Produce one Cost Baseline for the Cost Gate. Read [the adapted upstream reference](references/upstream.md) before applying it.

Return:

1. workload assumptions at 1x, 10x, and 100x;
2. twelve-month **Cash TCO** for providers, infrastructure, licenses, operations, migration, and exit;
3. separate **Effort Cost** for build, integration, operation, support, and future change;
4. primary cost drivers, thresholds, and uncertainty ranges;
5. at least two viable options when cost can change architecture;
6. lock-in, commitment, and data-exit exposure;
7. Budget Envelope fit, including `unknown`; and
8. facts, inferences, assumptions, source dates, and unresolved questions.

Use current primary-source research for prices, discounts, free tiers, limits, and provider capabilities. Treat static percentages and provider guidance as candidate questions, never current facts. An unknown Budget Envelope permits estimation but not paid deployment, annual commitments, or reserved-capacity purchases.

Keep the result advisory. Do not purchase, provision, resize, delete, commit spend, or present savings as realized.
