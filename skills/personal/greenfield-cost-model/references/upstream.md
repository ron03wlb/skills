Local adaptation expands cloud optimization into a greenfield decision model that includes human effort, migration, and exit costs.

## Reusable Guidance

Consider:

- visibility through allocation, budgets, forecasts, and ownership;
- workload fit and right-sizing;
- elasticity, idle resources, and lifecycle policies;
- on-demand, commitment, reserved, and interruptible pricing models;
- managed-service cost versus operational effort;
- storage, requests, observability, support, and data-transfer drivers;
- tagging or equivalent allocation standards where shared spend requires them; and
- lock-in, contract term, migration, and data-exit exposure.

Upstream percentage savings and provider examples are time-sensitive. Use them only to generate questions, then verify current prices, terms, regions, service coverage, interruption behavior, and limits from primary sources. Model uncertainty instead of claiming realized savings.
