# Greenfield Product Strategy

Produce one bounded Product Boundary packet for `start-project`. Read [the adapted upstream reference](references/upstream.md) before applying it.

Use existing conversation and discovery evidence first. Ask only for a missing answer that would change the boundary.

Return:

1. target user and buyer;
2. problem and available evidence;
3. desired outcome and measurable success;
4. value proposition and current alternatives;
5. MVP and explicit non-goals;
6. capabilities or partnerships required;
7. critical hypotheses and smallest validation actions; and
8. facts, inferences, assumptions, and unresolved questions.

Keep the packet advisory. Do not create a PRD, Matt Spec, acceptance criteria, roadmap, Tickets, implementation plan, or execution authorization. Mark market and competitor claims as assumptions unless supported by dated evidence.
