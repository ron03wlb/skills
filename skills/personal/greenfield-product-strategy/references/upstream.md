## Reusable Guidance

Test coherence across:

- vision and intended impact;
- segments defined by problems and jobs, with one first segment;
- cost position relative to alternatives;
- value proposition as before, mechanism, and after;
- explicit trade-offs and markets or features declined;
- one durable success measure and one near-term learning measure;
- acquisition and growth assumptions;
- required capabilities, partners, data, and AI safety where relevant; and
- defensibility through distribution, switching cost, network effects, data, or capability.

Surface the hypotheses that must be true and propose the smallest evidence-gathering action. Omit the upstream confirmation ceremony, quarterly roadmap posture, and any implication that this packet is a canonical product strategy or PRD.
