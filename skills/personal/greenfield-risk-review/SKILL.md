# Greenfield Risk Review

Run a compact pre-mortem for the Product Risk Gate. Read [the adapted upstream reference](references/upstream.md) when the verdict is not obvious.

Evaluate demand, positioning, monetization or strategic value, retention, trust, distribution, and adoption. Keep product risk distinct from engineering difficulty.

Return:

1. **Risk verdict:** low, medium, or high, with one reason;
2. **Main assumption:** the single assumption most likely to invalidate the project;
3. **Evidence:** what exists and what remains missing;
4. **Do next:** the smallest useful validation action and decision threshold;
5. **Delay:** what should wait for evidence; and
6. **Risk Triggers:** sensitive data, compliance, production migration, public contracts, availability or consistency promises, lock-in, material cost, or irreversible operations.

The verdict informs the gate; it does not authorize or forbid implementation. Keep missing facts visible instead of inventing market claims.
