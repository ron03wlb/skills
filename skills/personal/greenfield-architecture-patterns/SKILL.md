# Greenfield Architecture Patterns

Apply this specialist only after `start-project` records a Risk Trigger. Read [the adapted upstream reference](references/upstream.md) for pattern vocabulary.

Return:

1. the Risk Trigger and quality attribute that justify deeper design;
2. two credible architecture options, including the simplest viable option;
3. boundaries, dependency direction, data ownership, consistency, and failure isolation;
4. test seams and operational ownership;
5. migration, lock-in, and reversal cost;
6. rejected complexity and why it is premature; and
7. a recommendation with facts, inferences, assumptions, and unresolved questions.

Use Clean, Hexagonal, or Domain-Driven patterns only where they reduce a demonstrated coupling, testing, ownership, or change risk. Do not generate a project tree, implement interfaces, split services, or introduce enterprise controls by default. Accepted durable decisions enter an ADR only through Matt `domain-modeling`.
