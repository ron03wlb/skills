# Require a minimum Wiki page contract

Every Wiki topic page states its purpose and scope, current behavior, rules and invariants, dependencies, and sources. It also records states and exceptions when they apply.

The contract is independent of any generator so that human-authored and engine-proposed pages receive the same review. It requires meaningful content rather than empty sections. Material-claim mapping is defined by [ADR 0011](./0011-map-material-wiki-claims-to-sources.md), and the exact source format by [ADR 0012](./0012-use-stable-wiki-source-locators.md).
