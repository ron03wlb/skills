# Map material Wiki claims to sources

Each material claim in a Wiki topic page maps explicitly to one or more entries in that page's Sources section. Material claims cover current behavior, rules, invariants, states, exceptions, roles, permissions, ownership boundaries, and dependencies that could affect change planning or acceptance.

A page-level bibliography with no claim mapping does not satisfy this requirement. Explanatory transitions and other non-material prose do not require a citation, avoiding sentence-by-sentence citation noise.

The exact source-locator format is defined by [ADR 0012](./0012-use-stable-wiki-source-locators.md).
