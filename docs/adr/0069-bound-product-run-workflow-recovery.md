# Prevent recurring workflow faults and recover delivery automatically

Prevent known workflow failures from recurring by reconciling their verified fixes into the canonical source and checking the package actually selected by the host. Retain exact regressions for settled-result routing, stable request identity, interrupted cleanup and accepted-response loss. Full qualification belongs to the runtime release and relevant host capabilities; Run entry reuses its evidence with lightweight identity and capability checks. A passing source test does not prove that a different installed package has the repair. Qualification and performance observations do not promise failure-free execution.

Keep the authorized workflow responsible for diagnosis, repair, verification and automatic continuation. Repeated unchanged observations or a lack of verified progress trigger the next discriminating diagnosis or an existing supported escalation, rather than ending recovery merely because a timer expires. A known recoverable state uses its exact owner adapter; an unknown result is read back before retry; a source defect goes to one scoped isolated repair owner. That owner reproduces the failure, makes the smallest supported fix, runs affected checks and required review, and returns verified candidate/package/installation evidence. The active coordinator then resumes the original Run from the first unsatisfied stage under existing authority. Product workers and the main coordinator do not edit active governing packages. Same-fault retry and material-repair bounds remain cumulative; escalation changes the diagnostic method, not identity, authority or budget. Exhausted approved capacity or a genuinely missing decision remains attributable, without fabricating progress or silently abandoning independent work.

Reuse exact completed stages, verification inputs and repair outcomes rather than replaying implementation, successful merges or full histories. Partial cleanup resumes only proven remaining work. Matching approved maintenance ownership is reused across affected lanes when its source, scope and compatibility agree; no duplicate writer or guessed cross-Run authority is introduced. Retain one worktree per Issue, selected-batch capacity, repository-close-then-target-writer acquisition and reverse release. Healthy lease waiting is not a fault. Restore a failed merge before isolated conflict repair, and preserve a successful merge with failing integration checks. Only affected Issue/dependent work waits; unknown shared writer integrity gates its dependent mutations. No global scheduler, durable FIFO, machine-wide pool or cross-clone locking service is added.

Basis: in task `01a088f2-c5f5-75c2-84b4-9c0c380efa85` on 2026-09-10 the human clarified that the goal is to prevent faults and resolve them quickly, not stop the workflow because a fault occurred. This explicitly corrects the previous draft's interpretation of option C and withdraws its 300-second recovery-exhaustion policy and prohibition on automatic maintenance dispatch. Release-evidence reuse, progress-based diagnosis and focused verification are delegated reversible design choices. ADR-0038, ADR-0055, ADR-0057, ADR-0062 through ADR-0064, ADR-0066 and Spec #79 remain governing contracts; this ADR does not supersede their authorized autonomous repair. Spec #84 and its children retain their fault retry, ownership and execution-budget boundaries. Exact behavior and diagnostic targets belong to the revision-2 planning handoff.

Validation: replay the original routing, resume-identity, partial-cleanup and late-observation failures through the installed Windows boundary; prove that a repair reaches the effective package and the same Run finishes without duplicate effects or a redundant user restart. Distinguish progressing slow work from repeated unchanged faults. Measure detection, diagnosis, repair, verification and continuation separately, retaining maintenance, healthy waiting, blocked time and manual intervention in end-to-end elapsed time. Component tests or a preserved BLOCKED result cannot establish successful delivery or savings. Revisit thresholds and repair strategies only from new evidence; preserve historical Grants, requests and results.
