## What it does

`clarify-needs` helps turn a vague idea into a goal you can confirm, a reasoned comparison of ways to reach it, and a reviewable Needs Summary. The conversation converges when there is enough evidence to decide and a clear way to test what remains uncertain.

## When to reach for it

Type `/clarify-needs`, or the [agent](https://www.aihero.dev/ai-coding-dictionary/agent) reaches for it automatically when a task fits.

| Your situation | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| You have an idea such as “build a new notes app” but cannot yet say what it should improve | `clarify-needs` separates the proposed solution from your goal. |
| You want to pressure-test a plan through its decision tree | [grilling](https://aihero.dev/skills-grilling) explores the plan's branches. |
| You want to check your understanding against specified evidence | [confirm-understanding](https://aihero.dev/skills-confirm-understanding) calibrates that mental model. |
| You have a clear execution request or only need formatting | Continue that task directly. |

## First principles, then a choice

A Proposal Logic Chain makes the connection from action to goal visible. If you propose a reminder bot to prevent missed decisions, it asks what reminders would change and what supports that link. A sample in which four of five missed decisions lack an owner suggests an upstream hypothesis; it does not prove the cause.

A Fundamental Alternative might use an owner-and-decision convention in your existing tool. Both options face the same success criteria and constraints, such as no paid tools or after-hours notifications, while accounting for preferences such as asynchronous work. A more direct approach still has trade-offs. New evidence or a changed goal reopens the relevant reasoning, while settled answers carry forward.

## It's working if

- When you cannot explain what the notes app should improve, you get concrete situations to choose from, one important decision at a time.
- A possible goal change stays tentative until you accept it; once accepted, the comparison uses the new goal.
- You can tell a sample observation, a preference, and a causal assumption apart, and see what a small check could change.
- The final Needs Summary marks an unrun check as unverified. “Understanding is correct” does not initiate new work; a request to stop yields a partial summary with open questions.

## Where it fits

`clarify-needs` is a reach-for-it-anytime standalone conversation. [grilling](https://aihero.dev/skills-grilling) is its neighbour for plan pressure-testing, while [confirm-understanding](https://aihero.dev/skills-confirm-understanding) checks understanding against evidence. The summary creates shared understanding; existing explicit authorization for follow-up work carries forward to its applicable flow. [ask-matt](https://aihero.dev/skills-ask-matt) maps the wider skill set.
